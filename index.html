Ignacio Martín Huth

Ignacio Martín Huth is an Argentine entrepreneur active in the logistics and international trade sector. He is the director of Cargomann, a company specialized in cross-border road transportation and international logistics, and Textiles Barcelona, focused on the import and distribution of high-quality fabrics.

His professional activity is primarily based between Argentina and Brazil, with a strong focus on foreign trade operations, ground logistics, and business management within the Mercosur region.
